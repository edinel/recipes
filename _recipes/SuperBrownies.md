ingredients:
    - "1 cup unsalted butter"
    - "1 cup unsweetened cocoa powder"
    - "2 cups sugar"
    - "4 eggs"
    - "¾ cup all-purpose flour"
    - "¼ cup cake flour (optional - if not available use a total of 1 cup all-purpose flour)"
    - "1 Tbsp. vanilla"
    - "1 tsp. kosher salt"
    - "1 (12 oz.) pkg. good chocolate chips"
    - "1 cup chopped nuts (optional - I hate nuts in brownies)"
       
    
directions:
    - "Preheat oven to 350."
    - "Grease 13 x 9 x 2 pan."
    -  "Melt the butter over low heat (or microwave); pour into large mixing bowl."
    - "Add the cocoa, and stir until well blended."
    - "Add the sugar, and mix well. Add the eggs, one at a time, beating well after after each addition."
    - "Add the flour, vanilla, and salt and stir until just mixed together. *Do not overbeat.*"
    - "Stir in the chocolate chips and (optional nuts.)"
    - "Spread in the pan and bake 30 to 35 minutes."
    - "Let cool on a rack for 10 minutes. Cut into squares."
 
---
I learned this recipe from a friend in college.  They're really good -- I like to use either peanut butter or white chocolate chips instead of chocolate chips.
These brownies freeze well wrapped in plastic wrap.
